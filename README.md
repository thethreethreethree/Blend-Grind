Blend & Grind is a café and casual dining spot located in Pops District, Corong-Corong, El Nido, Palawan, Philippines. Known for its vibrant, laid-back atmosphere, it caters to both tourists and locals seeking quality coffee, smoothie bowls, and healthy meals in a relaxing tropical setting.

Atmosphere and Concept
Blend & Grind brands itself as a “good vibes eatery,” emphasizing comfort, friendliness, and community. The café’s modern, Instagram-ready interiors and casual outdoor seating make it a favorite among digital nomads, travelers, and locals. Its environment is noted for strong Wi-Fi, ample plugs, and a cowork-friendly layout ideal for remote work or study sessions.

Food and Beverage Offerings
The menu includes specialty coffee, fresh juices, smoothie bowls, and all-day breakfast items such as shakshuka, avocado toast, omelets, and pancakes. Guests highlight its selection of vegetarian and vegan dishes, while heartier options—like burgers, pasta, and savory waffles—round out the menu. Coffee drinks are made with quality beans and alternative milk choices, catering to diverse preferences.

Reputation and Reviews
Highly rated on travel platforms, Blend & Grind draws praise for its friendly staff, fast Wi-Fi, and consistent quality of food and beverages. Reviewers on Tripadvisor and Google describe it as one of El Nido’s best breakfast and brunch spots, appreciated for its cozy interiors, attentive service, and reasonable prices. Occasional feedback mentions peak-hour crowding, but overall sentiment remains strongly positive.

Community and Operations
Owned by Edilbert Canilla, the café operates as a small, independent business with under 50 employees. It supports local hiring and often posts recruitment for baristas and cooks. Blend & Grind’s mix of modern café culture and Palawan charm has made it a staple hangout for travelers exploring Corong-Corong’s beaches and nearby attractions.
